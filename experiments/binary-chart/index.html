How this playground works

Paper stakes, balances and payouts are in sats. The price chart stays in USDT. No wallet, orders or money. Cell multipliers are illustrative and don’t come from a market maker. Ranges include their lower bound and exclude their upper bound. Paper outcomes use the first source tick at or after expiry, within 2 seconds; missing prices void the trade and return the paper stake. Changing feeds resets the paper session. Live prices are BTC/USDT from Binance, not a USD index.
